# Research: city builders and what Capital Street takes from them

## SimCity BuildIt: how it works

- **Levels and XP.** You earn XP by expanding homes, building and upgrading services, finishing challenges, and building and upgrading special buildings. Each level unlocks rewards, features or new buildings, for example City Storage at level 4. ([EA help](https://help.ea.com/de/articles/simcity/simcity-buildit/rewards/), [fandom wiki](https://simcity.fandom.com/wiki/City_storage), [fan wiki level table](https://wikiwiki.jp/simcitybuild/%E3%83%AC%E3%83%99%E3%83%AB))
- **The production loop.** Factories make raw materials (metal at level 1, wood at 2, plastic at 6, textiles at 15, glass at 19, animal feed at 23). Stores craft them into goods. Goods upgrade homes, and upgraded homes bring population, cash and XP. The number of factory slots grows with level. ([factory resources guide](https://www.appgamer.com/simcity-buildit/strategy-guide/factory-resources), [industrial buildings](https://simcity.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_industrial_buildings_in_SimCity_BuildIt), [EA forum tips](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/simcity-buildit-en/simcity-buildit---tip-and-tricks-guide-wip/10382007))
- **Homes.** A yellow hard hat over a home means it can be upgraded. Each upgrade step raises population, roughly +104, +154, +313, +752, +1,836. ([EA beginner guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/simcity/simcity-buildit/beginner-guide/), [Green Valley zone](https://simcity-buildit.fandom.com/wiki/Green_Valley_Zone))
- **Services.** Power, water, sewage and waste work by capacity. Fire, police and health work by coverage area. Sims get unhappy when services fall short. Guides disagree on unlock levels: fire around 5, police 8 to 12, health around 16. ([services](https://simcity-buildit.fandom.com/wiki/Services?oldid=4190), [services guide](https://www.appgamer.com/simcity-buildit/strategy-guide/services))
- **Regions.** Extra themed satellite cities open from level 25, then at 250,000 and 1,000,000 total regional population. ([EA forum: regions](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/simcity-buildit-en/simcity-buildit---all-about-regions/10467983), [regions wiki](https://simcity-buildit.fandom.com/wiki/Regions))
- **HUD.** Money (several currencies) sits top-right, with level and population alongside, and the build menu sits at the lower right with streets, homes, shops, factories and specialisations. ([ludo.guide game screen](https://www.ludo.guide/guide/simcity/game-screen), [EA forum tips](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/simcity-buildit-en/simcity-buildit---tip-and-tricks-guide-wip/10382007))
- **Look.** A full 3D city you can spin and zoom, with highly detailed buildings borrowed from the look of the PC SimCity. Reviewers say the presentation is the main draw. ([PocketGamer hands-on](https://www.pocketgamer.com/simcity-buildit/hands-on-with-simcity-buildit-eas-latest-free-to-play-reimagining/), [TouchArcade review](https://toucharcade.com/2014/12/23/simcity-buildit-review/))
- **Common complaint.** Timers and micromanagement. One player called the build menu "too many taps". ([PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/simcity-buildit/hands-on-with-simcity-buildit-eas-latest-free-to-play-reimagining/), [itch.io feedback](https://itch.io/post/8297296), [strategygame.org](https://strategygame.org/the-best-mobile-city-building-games-that-respect-your-thumbs/))

## Other city builders and UI trends

- **Pocket City 2** is praised for road drawing that follows your finger, a build menu you can reach one-handed, and no ads or timers. ([strategygame.org](https://strategygame.org/the-best-mobile-city-building-games-that-respect-your-thumbs/), [TouchArcade review](https://toucharcade.com/2023/04/18/pocket-city-2-review-mobile-iphone-ipad-premium-city-builder/))
- **2026 mobile UI trends:** fewer on-screen elements with bigger touch targets, controls within thumb reach, and bold silhouettes. Art leans to stylised, rounded "toy" 3D, with some glass-panel UI. ([AAA Game Art Studio](https://aaagameartstudio.com/blog/mobile-games-art), [Pixune](https://pixune.com/blog/best-examples-mobile-game-ui-design/), [Sunstrike](https://sunstrikestudios.com/en/blog/mobile_game_industry_2025/), [AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/blog/mobile-gaming-trends-2026))

## What Capital Street uses

- **Same kinds of systems:** levels and XP, building families unlocked by level, a factory → store → home loop, services by capacity and coverage, and new regions as you grow. Our regions are world cities on the zoomed-out map.
- **Our own names and art.** SimCity, its currency names (Simoleons, SimCash) and its artwork belong to EA. Our money is cash and gold, and every icon and building drawing is ours.
- **HUD in the BuildIt layout:** level star and XP bar top-left, population centre, money top-right, a big Build button bottom-right that opens category tabs with building cards. The Market button bottom-left is where Capital Street's trading lives.
- **From the trends:** big thumb-reach buttons, few elements on screen, chunky "toy" styling, glass panels over the 3D city.
- **Avoiding the complaints:** short starter tasks so the first levels come quickly, and as few taps as possible to build.
