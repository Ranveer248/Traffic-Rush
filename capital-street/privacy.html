Capital Street Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

Capital Street is a trading and business game, on the web, on PC and in app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store). Playing alone, it collects nothing about you. Playing online is optional: then the server keeps only what the shared world needs (your player name, your buildings and your chat messages) and an email address only if you choose to add one. There are no ads, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold.

Playing alone

  • Your saved game (cash, shares, loans, buildings, office and staff) is kept on your own device only. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere.

Playing online (only if you choose to)

When you press Play online, the game connects to its server and keeps:

  • An account ID. As a guest this is a random number tied to your device; nothing else about you is needed.
  • Your email address and a protected (hashed) password, only if you sign up with an email or add one to a guest account, so you can sign in on other devices. We use it only for signing in and resetting your password, never for marketing.
  • Your player name and colour, which everyone sees on your buildings, in chat and on the rich list. Don't use your real name.
  • Which buildings you own in each city, and how many floors, so no two players can own the same one.
  • Your chat messages, which other players can read. Don't post personal information.
  • Your net worth and current city, reported by your game for the rich list, and when you were last online.
  • While you play, other players can see that you're online and which city you're in.

Like any website, the server sees your IP address when your game connects, and keeps it in short-lived security logs to stop abuse. It is not used to identify or track you.

Who runs the server

The online part runs on Supabase, which stores the data above for us and processes it only on our instructions (Supabase privacy policy). Nobody else gets it.

Deleting your account

In the game, open Online, then Account, then Delete my account. This immediately deletes your login, player, buildings and messages from the server. Your saved game on your device stays, and you can keep playing alone. Accounts that are never used again may also be deleted after a year.

What the game never does

  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers.
  • No real money: every market, company and price in the game is fictional. Nothing in it is financial advice.

Network

The app works offline with everything it needs inside it. The website version downloads the game's files, including map data, from the site that hosts it, like any web page. Online play connects to the server described above.

Children

Online play follows the age rules in the Terms: 13 and over, or 16 in the EU and UK unless a parent or guardian agrees. Younger players can play alone, which collects nothing. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can see your data in the game, change your name, and delete everything at any time. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may also have the right to ask for a copy of it or to complain to a data protection authority.

Map data

Maps © OpenStreetMap contributors and the Overture Maps Foundation. Country shapes from Natural Earth. Everything the game uses from others is listed on the Credits page; the rules for playing are in the Terms of Service.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on the game's GitHub repository.
