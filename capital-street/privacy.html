Capital Street Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version

Capital Street is a trading and business game, on the web and in app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store). It collects nothing about you, has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and never sells or shares anything.

What the game stores

  • Your saved game (cash, shares, loans, buildings, office and staff) is kept on your own device only. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-in, no personal information, no location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers.
  • No real money: every market, company and price in the game is fictional. Nothing in it is financial advice.

Network

The app works offline with everything it needs inside it. The website version downloads the game's files, including map data, from the site that hosts it, like any web page.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Map data

Maps © OpenStreetMap contributors and the Overture Maps Foundation. Country shapes from Natural Earth.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on the game's GitHub repository.
