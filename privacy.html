Cargo Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version

Cargo Run is a free driving game. It collects as little as possible, never shows ads, and never sells or shares your information.

What the game stores

  • Your game progress (money, cars, maps, settings) is saved in your own browser on your device.
  • Your driver name and scores are sent to the online leaderboard when you set a new best, so other players can see them. Choose a driver name that isn't your real full name.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can play without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Google, the game receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google. They are used only to:

  • keep your account signed in, and
  • save your game progress online so you can play on more than one device.

Your email address is never shown to other players.

Where the information is kept

Online data (the leaderboard and saved progress) is stored with Supabase, the database service the game uses. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

What we never do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • We never sell or give your information to anyone else.
  • We never ask for payment details.

Deleting your information

To delete your saved progress on a device, clear the site's data in your browser settings. To have your online data (scores or signed-in account) deleted, contact us through the game's GitHub page below and we will remove it.

Children

If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before signing in with Google.

Contact

Questions? Get in touch through the game's GitHub page: github.com/Ranveer248/cargo-run
